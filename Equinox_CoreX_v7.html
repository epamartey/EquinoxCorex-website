What We Supply

Our Products

A comprehensive range of high-quality energy, electrical, and industrial products — engineered for reliability, safety, and performance across Ghana and Africa.

Our smart metering product line offers utilities a complete hardware and software ecosystem for AMI deployment — from individual meters to network-wide data management systems.

Smart Electricity Meters

High-accuracy single & three-phase smart meters. DLMS/COSEM and IEC 62056 compliant. Remote reading, tamper detection and load control.

Prepaid Electricity Meters

STS-compliant prepaid meters for token-based electricity payment. Single and three-phase with keypad and smart card interfaces.

Data Concentrator Units (DCU)

Network aggregation devices collecting, processing, and transmitting meter data. Supports PLC, RF, GPRS, and NB-IoT communication technologies.

Communication Modules

Plug-in modules supporting PLC, RF mesh, GPRS/3G/4G, and NB-IoT for flexible AMI network architectures suited to local infrastructure conditions.

Energy Storage Batteries

Lithium-based floor-standing and wall-mounted energy storage for solar hybrid systems and backup power. Scalable residential to utility-scale.

Energy Management Software (MDM)

Head End System and MDM platform for billing analytics, commercial loss detection, load profiling, and consumer segmentation reporting.

Energy Meter Enclosures

Weatherproof and tamper-resistant single & three-phase meter enclosures for outdoor and indoor utility and distribution deployments.

Energy Meter Test Benches

Precision calibration and testing equipment for single and three-phase electricity meters. Verifies accuracy against international metrological standards.

We supply a comprehensive range of high-voltage and medium-voltage electrical equipment for power transmission, distribution, and substation applications.

Power Transformers

Oil-immersed and dry-type power transformers for transmission and distribution networks. Wide voltage class range for utility, industrial, and renewable applications.

Distribution Transformers

Medium and low voltage distribution transformers for grid-connected and off-grid power supply. Compact, efficient designs suited to African network conditions.

High-Voltage Switchgear & Breakers

Air-insulated and gas-insulated (GIS) switchgear, circuit breakers, disconnectors, and earthing switches for transmission and distribution substations.

Surge Arresters

Metal oxide surge arresters (MOSAs) for overvoltage protection of transformers, switchgear, cables, and grid assets at all voltage levels.

Capacitors & Filters

Shunt capacitor banks, filter capacitors, and harmonic filter systems for power factor correction and power quality improvement in networks and industrial facilities.

Cable Accessories & Connectors

HV and MV cable joints, terminations, connectors, and accessories for underground and overhead cable systems at all voltage levels.

Advanced energy storage and power conversion products for grid-scale, commercial, and industrial applications.

Battery Energy Storage Systems (BESS)

Grid-scale and commercial BESS for peak shaving, frequency regulation, renewable energy integration, and backup power. Modular and scalable architecture.

Solar PV Inverters & Converters

String inverters, central inverters, and hybrid inverters for solar PV systems across residential, commercial, and utility scales.

HVDC Equipment

High-Voltage Direct Current converter stations and components for long-distance power transmission and offshore grid interconnections.

FACTS Systems

Flexible AC Transmission Systems including Static VAR Compensators (SVCs) and STATCOMs for voltage stability and reactive power management.

Static Frequency Converters (SFC)

Frequency conversion systems for industrial, traction, and test applications requiring independent frequency control independent of the grid.

Integrated protection, automation, and control products for digital substations and intelligent power networks.

Protection Relays

Numerical protection relays for line, transformer, busbar, generator, and motor protection. IEC 61850-compliant for digital substation integration.

Substation Automation Systems

Integrated automation hardware and software including Bay Control Units (BCUs), Remote Terminal Units (RTUs), and IEC 61850 gateways.

Communication Networks for Energy

Industrial-grade fibre optic systems, Ethernet switches, and cybersecurity hardware for energy operational technology (OT) environments.

SCADA & Remote Monitoring

Supervisory Control and Data Acquisition hardware and software for real-time monitoring and control of power networks and industrial facilities.
